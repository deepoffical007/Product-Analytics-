# Decision Log

Format: decision · options considered · choice and why · trade-off · status.
Status: `Proposed` (AI-drafted, candidate not yet confirmed) · `Confirmed` (candidate agreed) · `Revised` · `Rejected`.

> Every `Confirmed` decision must be one the candidate can defend unaided in the 30–45 min walkthrough.

| ID | Decision | Options considered | Choice and why | Trade-off | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Levels will be named by impact (e.g., hygiene risk vs service gap) and defined by observable rules. | Slightly less familiar to engineers; needs a definition table | Proposed |

| D-12 | Classification method | (a) Pick from a flat list; (b) Ordered decision questions | **(b)** Order resolves the sample traps: status first (S-04), account second (S-05), billing third (S-02), failure vs new request last (S-01 vs S-03). | Slightly slower for the logger; can be a guided picker in the form | Proposed (Phase 1) |
| D-13 | Mixed messages (S-03) | (a) One case, primary type only; (b) Split only independent needs into linked cases; (c) Always split per sentence | **(b)** Independent needs have different owners and closure conditions; dependent needs (e.g., a credit note *if* a miss is confirmed) stay as actions inside the primary case. | Linked cases add admin; the "independent" judgement needs examples in training | Proposed (Phase 1) |
| D-14 | Repeats | Auto-merge vs suggest | **Suggest; a person decides** between status enquiry / duplicate / recurrence / unrelated. Auto-merge would hide recurrences, which are the C-02 signal. | Depends on logger judgement; decisions are logged | Proposed (Phase 1) |
| D-15 | Intake depth | (a) Full form at first contact; (b) Two steps: Log (minimum fields, by the receiver) → Triage (complete, by CS) | **(b)** If logging is slower than a WhatsApp reply, people won't log, and C-01 returns. | A record is briefly incomplete; Triage SLA must be short (Phase 2) | Proposed (Phase 1) |
| D-16 | Owner model | One owner vs resolution owner + communication owner | **Both fields.** Resolution owner changes on handoff (C-04); the communication owner (CS) stays stable, so the customer has one voice (C-07). | D-17 | Severity scope | Severity on all types vs not on service requests | **Not on service requests**: they have a requested-by date. A current impact becomes a linked operational complaint (S-03). | Requests with real urgency rely on the linked complaint | Proposed (Phase 1) |
| D-18 | S-02 closure | Corrected invoice only vs invoice + master data | **Both.** Otherwise the October invoice repeats the error. | Needs an owner for site master data (unknown) | Proposed (Phase 1) |
| D-19 | Severity levels | Generic tiers vs impact-named levels | **Hygiene-Critical / Service-Impacting / Low-Impact**, each with an observable test, plus three escalators (recurrence, 3+ sites, overdue ≥ 3 days). Seniority, tone and revenue are not inputs (C-08). | Three levels are coarse; thresholds need calibration with real data | Proposed (Phase 2) |
| D-20 | Priority | Separate manual priority tiers vs a computed queue order | **Computed order:** severity → SLA state → flags (rejected, chased, recurrence) → age. | D-21 | Ownership during a handoff | Transfers on assignment vs on acceptance | **On acceptance.** The sender stays accountable until the receiver accepts or returns it. | D-22 | Escalation on breach | Auto-reassign vs required decision | **The escalation contact must record a decision** (reassign / take over / new date with reason). Auto-reassignment just moves the problem. | Relies on managers acting; their decision time is itself measured | Proposed (Phase 2) |
| D-23 | Customer visibility without a portal | Portal vs CS-driven updates | **CS updates at defined points plus a "maximum silence" timer (R6)** per severity. A portal is deferred (A-06). | CS effort; depends on discipline, so R6 breaches are tracked | Proposed (Phase 2) |
| D-24 | Non-response at confirmation | Keep open vs auto-close | **Two reminders, then `Closed – No Response` at 5 business days**, excluded from the confirmed-closure rate; 14-day reopen window. | Some unresolved issues close silently; the reopen window and the SE-confirmation option limit this | Proposed (Phase 2) |
| D-25 | SLA clock start | Logged at vs received at | **Received at.** Logging delay counts against PadCare and stays visible (C-01). | Received time is entered by the logger, so it can be wrong; backdating is allowed but audited | Proposed (Phase 2) |

| D-28 | How to present walkthrough findings | (a) Silently fix earlier phases; (b) Show the run with ⚠ markers, list fixes, update Task 1 marked † | **(b)** The brief rewards catching flaws ("self-critique is gold") *and* demands consistency.
